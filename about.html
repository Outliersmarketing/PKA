Who We Are

Founded in April 2013

Pratik Karania and Associates (PKA) is an accounting, audit and consultancy firm serving businesses that value clear answers and practical advice.

Led by Pratik Karania, whose professional experience spans accounting practice, audit and in-house financial management, we understand both the technical requirements and the day-to-day decisions behind a business's numbers.

We work closely with clients to bring clarity to their finances and confidence to their next steps.

Founder's Note

A word from Pratik

“

Before founding PKA, I spent nearly eight years training and working across some of Kenya's respected accounting and audit practices — from finalizing year-end accounts and statutory returns at Ghedia & Co., to auditing private and large companies at Shantilal Gala & Co. and Vipin Shah & Associates, to sitting inside industry as a Group Financial Controller at Metal Crowns Ltd (Group of companies) and later as Financial Controller & Manager where I managed everything from credit control, finance and accounting, Hr and IT to ISO 9001/22000 implementation.

That mix of practice-side audit experience and in-house financial management is what shapes how PKA works today: we don't just audit your numbers from the outside — we understand how a business actually runs.
